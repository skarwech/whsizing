# G1 audit: Independent golden oracle (T1.5 golden case)

## Verdict: pass_with_issues

On the golden side, the work holds up. All 12 G1 tests pass, the G1 paths are clean under ruff, ruff format and pyright, the auditors reproduced the key golden values independently from the parquet files, and T5.3 now passes against `golden_values.json`.
The verdict is not upgraded to pass for four reasons. The engine-side T1.5 property (engine equals spreadsheet within 0.5 %) has no test and no skip placeholder. The spreadsheet only partly meets "every cell has a formula comment". The oracle has latent defects in eligibility and in the SLA window. All of these findings can still be reproduced on 2026-11-08.

Both round-0 auditors (spec and proof) returned `pass_with_issues`.

## Open issues

1. The engine-side T1.5 property is untested, and there is no skip placeholder (M1).
2. The oracle's F5 eligibility ignores missing dims or weight (they are zero-filled) and hazmat/temperature constraints, and a null `units_per_case` would crash `inbound()` (M6). This is latent on tiny_golden, but it is wrong on any other dataset.
3. An explicit `sla_window_hours` is ignored, and cutoff == departure divides by zero (L6). This is latent.
4. The SKUs and DailySeries sheets have no formula comments, some stage comments name a section rather than a formula, and the workbook holds no live Excel formulas (M2).
5. The golden flags are a subset of the F6 table: F_LOW_CONFIDENCE is missing, and F_DOCK_SHORTFALL and F_ELIG_ASSUMED are not evaluated. The omission is not registered in INTERPRETATIONS.md (M5).
6. The golden basis does not exercise the pallet-order, multi-parcel, shipping_pallet or overflow-shelving branches (L7).
7. The interpretations the engine must match, or that need an expert ruling: I11, I14, I19, I21, I22/I38, I23 (contradicts the F6b text), I24 (conflicts with the catalog comment), I32, I33, I34.
8. File size: `oracle.py` is 728 lines and `build_golden.py` is 746. Both are under the 800 maximum but above the 400 typical size.
9. Outside G1 paths: the whole suite has 1 failure, `tests/unit/test_f0_tooling.py::test_T0_1_lint_and_typecheck_gates_pass[ruff-check]`, caused by E501 at `src/whsizing/schemas/params.py:40` (F0/F5 code). It is not a G1 failure and is not counted in the G1 totals.

## Not applicable here and why

- **Engine-side T1.5 comparison:** the F3/F4/F6/F7 engine packages are 0-byte stubs. The comparison belongs to T6.1-T6.3 and T7.1 once those features exist. Only F5 (T5.3) consumes `golden_values.json` today, and it passes.
- **Expert rulings** on I19 (roster floor), I33 (temp hours counted twice), I24 (pack-station pooling), I11 (pick-face positions) and I23 (decant): these need a domain expert and cannot be settled by a test.
- **F6e queue values** (Erlang-C on pack stations and ports): not in the golden values, because the build step did not ask for them. `erlang_c` and `erlang_wq` are in `oracle.py` and are unit-tested.
- **Multi-shift rosters:** the golden basis uses one shift, and the oracle raises NotImplementedError for more than one (I19).
- **Lo/hi worlds:** the oracle computes the typical world only.
- **S1 amr_gtp:** the golden case covers S0 and S2 only.
- **Docker and real client data:** not used by G1. The golden case runs on the synthetic `tests/golden/tiny_golden` data only.
