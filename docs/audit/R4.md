## Open issues

None is high or medium. None changes a golden number.

1. **F6 recasts the new warning flags to `info` (low, outside R4 paths).** `engine/size.py:164` applies `model_copy(update={"severity": "info"})` to every design-day flag. `F_INBOUND_SPARSE` and `F_RECEIPTS_NO_PALLETS` are warnings in `Inbound.flags`, `DesignDay.flags` and `design_day.json` and `info` in `result.json`. I70 names only `F_SHORT_HISTORY`, `F_RATIO_DRIFT` and `F_PERCENTILE_CLIPPED` as `info`. Decision for the F6 owner: keep `warning` for these two codes if they should stay visible, and add a test at the result level.
2. **Stale register and audit records (low, outside R4 paths).** `docs/INTERPRETATIONS.md` should (a) add to the I71 sentence that a P_p of 0 over sparse receipts now raises `F_INBOUND_SPARSE`, (b) register `F_INBOUND_SPARSE` (warning, value = receipt days among the candidate days) and `F_RECEIPTS_NO_PALLETS` (warning, value = receipt days), and (c) extend the I46 flag order to design-day flags, `F_INBOUND_SPARSE`, `F_RECEIPTS_NO_PALLETS`, then `F_DEFAULT_UPP`. `docs/audit/F7.md`, `F11.md` and `R3.md` still list the findings in the table above as open; this record supersedes them for those items.
3. **A 90 to 100 % coverage month is used without a signal (low).** Under I74 (e) it counts as complete, but a mid-month cut can shift the peak-month error by several points with `accepted True` and no flag (proof 6). Decision needed: leave as the spec's `[P]` threshold, or add an info flag or a note in the calibration output.
